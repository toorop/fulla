## Is Fulla free?

Yes. Fulla is free and open source (MIT licence). You pay the AI service you connect, per image, from about 5 cents.

## Do I need a Photoshop subscription?

You need Photoshop 2026 (27.11 or later). Fulla doesn't use Adobe's generative credits.

## Do I need the Creative Cloud app?

On macOS, yes: Fulla installs through it. On Windows, no: the installer works without it. See [Install](/docs/install/).

## Where do my photos go?

Straight from Photoshop to the AI service you chose. There is no Fulla server. See [Privacy and security](/docs/privacy/).

## Which model should I choose?

Nano Banana 2.1, the default. See [Models](/docs/models/) for the others.

## Can I use GPT Image?

Yes, through OpenRouter or with an OpenAI key.

## Does Fulla work offline?

No. The images are made by an online AI service.
