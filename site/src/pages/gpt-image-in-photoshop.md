GPT Image is OpenAI's family of image models. With Fulla, a free Photoshop plugin, you can use them for generative fill: select an area, describe what you want, and click Generate. You pay per image, through OpenRouter or with your own OpenAI key.

## Which GPT Image models does Fulla offer?

| Model | Through OpenRouter | With an OpenAI key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GPT Image 2.6 | Yes | No |
| GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst | Yes | Yes (default) |
| GPT Image 2.5 Flare | No | Yes |
| GPT Image 2 | No | Yes |

OpenAI models make images up to 2K. Fulla's recommended model stays Nano Banana 2.1, which in our tests kept faces closer to the original; GPT Image is there when you prefer its look.

## Set up GPT Image in Photoshop with Fulla

1. [Install Fulla](/docs/install/) in Photoshop 2026 (27.11 or later).
2. Connect OpenRouter from the panel, or add an OpenAI key. See [First run](/docs/first-run/).
3. Pick a GPT Image model with the model button under the prompt, then select, describe and click **Generate**.

With an OpenAI key, your account needs credits, and OpenAI image models may need your organization verified.
