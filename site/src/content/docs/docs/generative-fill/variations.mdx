Fulla can make **1 to 4 variations** in one run, so you can pick the best.

## Ask for variations

1. Click the **settings button** (the sliders) next to the model button.
2. Set **Variations** to 2, 3 or 4.
3. Click **Generate**.

## Pick one

Thumbnails appear in the card. Click one to put it in the layer; it is one undo step, and you can try each in turn. Click **Done** once you have chosen: the card, the thumbnails and the prompt are cleared, and the layer stays.
