Fulla is a free Photoshop plugin that works like Photoshop's own generative fill: select, describe, click Generate. The difference is who makes the images and how you pay for them. With Photoshop's generative fill, Adobe makes them and you pay in generative credits from your plan. With Fulla, an AI service you choose makes them, and you pay that service per image.

## The short answer

- **Choose Fulla** if you want to pay only for the images you make, see each image's cost in dollars, use models Photoshop doesn't offer, or get results up to 4K.
- **Keep Photoshop's own tool** for quick fills with Firefly Fill & Expand, which costs 1 credit, or if your plan already gives you more credits than you use.

## Price

Photoshop's generative fill is paid in generative credits. Each plan includes a number of credits per month, which don't carry over, and more credits are sold as monthly add-ons. What a fill costs depends on the model and the size: 1 credit with Firefly Fill & Expand, 20 credits with Nano Banana 2, 40 with Nano Banana Pro. At the smallest add-on, $9.99 for 2,000 credits, a credit is worth half a cent, so a Nano Banana 2 fill is about $0.10. The full detail is in [Photoshop generative credits, explained](/photoshop-generative-credits/).

With Fulla, you pay the AI service per image, at its price. With Nano Banana 2.1, Fulla's recommended model, we measured $0.048 for a 1K image and $0.063 for a 2K one on 10 November 2026. The panel shows the cost of every run; with OpenRouter, it is the exact amount billed.

## Models

Photoshop offers Adobe's Firefly models and a few partner models. Fulla offers a tested list from several services, and adds a model when it does better or costs less. See [Models](/docs/models/).

## Resolution

Adobe's Firefly Image 5 makes images up to 2K, about 4 megapixels; beyond that, Adobe offers a separate Generative Upscale, paid in credits. Fulla's Auto resolution asks for about twice the size of your selection, up to 4K when the model offers it. See [Resolution](/docs/generative-fill/resolution/).

## Privacy

With Photoshop's generative fill, your image goes to Adobe. With Fulla, it goes from Photoshop straight to the AI service you chose: there is no Fulla server. See [Privacy and security](/docs/privacy/).

## How we compared

- Same need on both sides: generative fill inside Photoshop.
- Same model for prices: Nano Banana 2, at the same sizes.
- Adobe's prices are converted at the price of its smallest credit add-on. Fulla's are measured runs.
- Every figure is dated. When a price changes, this page is updated.
