A few habits make the difference between a fill that shows and one that doesn't.

## Select generously

Leave room around what you add or remove.

To add an object, the model fits it to your selection: **give the selection the proportions of the finished object, shadow included**. With the wrong proportions, the object comes out cut at the edge of the selection, most often its shadow. In low, raking light the shadow is long: widen the selection on the side where it falls.

## Ask for the light, the perspective and the depth of field

The model doesn't always guess them. Add them to your prompt: `put the mug from ref 1 on the table, match the light, the perspective and the depth of field`.

## Describe the change, not the photo

Say what should appear or change in the selection, not the style of the whole photo: Fulla already sends the photo around it.

## Use a reference for what was really there

A face, an object, a sign: give the model a photo of it. See [Reference images](/docs/generative-fill/reference-images/).

## Generate again when a result misses

Models draw at random, and about one result in four can miss. Click **Generate** again, or ask for [variations](/docs/generative-fill/variations/) and pick the best.
