---
title: Tone matching
description: Fulla compares the result with your photo around the selection and adds a Curves filter when the tones drift.
---

Models often shift the colours of what they return. Fulla compares the result with your photo around the selection and, when needed, adds a **Curves** smart filter to the new layer that brings the tones back.

## Adjust or turn it off

The filter is an ordinary smart filter on the layer: hide it to compare, or open it to adjust the curves. Nothing is added when the tones already match.

For colours to match in documents that aren't sRGB, Photoshop must keep embedded profiles: see [Colours](/docs/colours/).
