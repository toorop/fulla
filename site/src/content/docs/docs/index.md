Fulla is a free, open-source Photoshop plugin for generative fill. You select an area, say what you want, and click **Generate**, as with Photoshop's own generative fill. Fulla uses your own account with an AI service, and you pay that service per image: no subscription, no credits.

## Where to start

1. [Install Fulla](/docs/install/) in Photoshop 2026 (27.11 or later).
2. [Connect an AI service](/docs/first-run/): OpenRouter takes a few clicks from the panel.
3. [Make your first fill](/docs/generative-fill/).

## Get better results

- [Remove and extend](/docs/generative-fill/remove-and-extend/): erase what you select, or fill a larger canvas.
- [Reference images](/docs/generative-fill/reference-images/): show the model what you mean.
- [Getting good results](/docs/generative-fill/tips/): how to select and what to ask for.

Something wrong? See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) and the [FAQ](/docs/faq/).
