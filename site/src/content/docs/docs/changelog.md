## 0.1.0

Fulla's first release: generative fill in Photoshop 2026 (27.11 or later), through your own account with [OpenRouter](/docs/providers/), OpenAI or Google. Select an area, describe what you want, and click Generate.

- [Models](/docs/models/): Nano Banana 2.1 (recommended) and Nano Banana Pro, Qwen Image 3 and 2.1 Pro, GPT Image 2.6, 2.5 Sunburst, 2.5 Flare and 2.
- [Automatic resolution](/docs/generative-fill/resolution/), up to 4K.
- Up to 8 [reference images](/docs/generative-fill/reference-images/) per document, and the whole photo sent along as a scene reference when the selection is small.
- Automatic [colour](/docs/colours/) and tone matching, so a fill blends with the photo around it.
- The cost of every run shown in dollars, no subscription.
- [Install](/docs/install/) with the Creative Cloud app on Windows and macOS, or a Windows-only installer that needs no administrator rights.
