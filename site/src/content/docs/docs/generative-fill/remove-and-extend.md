---
title: Remove and extend
description: Remove what you select with an empty prompt, or enlarge the canvas and let Fulla fill the new area from the photo.
---

With an **empty prompt**, Fulla removes what you select and fills the area from what surrounds it. The same works for an empty area after you enlarge the canvas.

## Remove something

1. Select the object or person to remove, with a little room around it, and its shadow if it has one.
2. Leave the prompt empty.
3. Click **Generate**.

## Extend a photo

1. Enlarge the canvas in Photoshop (**Image > Canvas Size**).
2. Select the new, empty area, overlapping the photo a little.
3. Leave the prompt empty and click **Generate**.

You can fill both sides at once or one at a time. To add something in the new area, describe it instead of leaving the prompt empty.

:::tip
Select generously, with room around what you remove and its shadow.
:::
